Frequently Asked Questions

Below are answers to some of the most common questions we receive at our Leeds practice.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

Do you accept walk-in appointments?

We recommend booking in advance, but we do accept limited walk-ins for urgent issues.

How can I register as a new patient?

You can register online through our patient portal or by visiting our reception with a valid ID and proof of address.

Can I request prescriptions online?

Yes! Registered patients can request repeat prescriptions through our secure online system or via the NHS app.

Where are you located?

We’re located at 12 Park Row, Leeds LS1 5HU — near Leeds City Centre. Parking is available on site.
